QuickMail v0.8.57 Release Notes

Released October 15, 2026 — downloads for this release

New: hear how many messages in a conversation are unread

A conversation row could only say “Has unread”, which sounds the same whether one message of twenty is new or all twenty are. Two new fields in Message List Fields say how many: Unread count (“2 unread”) and Unread of total (“2 of 5 unread”). They work on conversation rows and on the group rows in the From and To views, say nothing when everything has been read, and are off until you turn one on, so your rows sound the same as before unless you choose otherwise. They start at the end of the row; use Move Up in Message List Fields to hear the count sooner (#800).

New: choose what goes between the fields of a message list row

Each row in the message list puts a period between its fields, and if you listen at a slow rate the pause at every period adds up over a long list. Message List Fields now has a Between fields choice: Period (the default, so nothing changes unless you choose), Comma, or Space. Only Period ends the row with a period. Punctuation inside a field, such as the sentences of a preview, is left as it is. The choice applies to message rows, conversation rows and the group rows in the From and To views (#802).

New: choose the word each field says

Each field in Message List Fields now has a Spoken as box with the word it says: a state’s word (“unread”, “replied”), the word after a count (“3 messages”, “2 unread”), or a text field’s label when Speak field labels is on. Change it to a shorter word, or empty it to say nothing extra: a count then says just its number. With Unread count set to “of” just before an emptied Message count, and the Space separator, a conversation reads “6 of 12 Budget review”, or “12 Budget review” when it’s all read. Default word puts the shipped word back, and Reset to Defaults restores them all (#807).
